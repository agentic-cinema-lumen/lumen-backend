# 🎬 100 Included Cinema Releases

The complete benchmark dataset of 100 genuine feature films. Each movie package in `data/movies/` contains:
- Complete screenplay (`script.txt`)
- Real widescreen stills across 6 narrative beats (`keyframes/shot_0001.jpg` ... `shot_0006.jpg`)
- Narrative timeline manifest (`shot_manifest.json`)
- Metadata and benchmark IMDb ratings (`metadata.json`)
