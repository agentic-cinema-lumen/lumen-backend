THE LONG NIGHT - SPEC DRAFT (EXCERPT)

EXT. WINTERFELL - COURTYARD - NIGHT

Total, impenetrable blackness. The cold wind howls across the battlements.

JON SNOW stares into the swirling gloom, grip tight on Longclaw.

JON SNOW
The dead are already here.

BRIENNE OF TARTH
Stand your ground! Hold the line!

Across the snow-covered trench, shadowy shapes hurtle forward through the freezing fog.
Silent screams tear through the ranks.

INT. WINTERFELL - GREAT HALL - NIGHT

TYRION LANNISTER sits on a dusty barrel, nursing a goblet of sour wine.
SANSA STARK enters, her face pale in the flickering candlelight.

TYRION
If we were up there, maybe we would see something.

SANSA
We wouldn't see anything. It's pitch black out there.
You'd die faster than the rest of them.

TYRION
At least we would die seeing who killed us.

EXT. WINTERFELL - GODSWOOD - NIGHT

The Night King approaches BRAN STARK in his wheelchair.
The tempo accelerates. Blades clash in the shadows.
Suddenly, ARYA STARK leaps from the darkness!
In a split second, the dagger switches hands.
Strike! The ice shatters into a million crystals.

THE END
